# Talent Campus v0.1 roadmap

## Release goal

Run a standalone, single-operator Talent Campus in which a user defines a role, develops an AI persona or registers a human candidate, prepares a consideration, sends it through the PostOffice to a designated Decisions office, and sees the returned decision. Records must survive restart. Integration into the main Forge campus follows this release.

## Milestones and acceptance criteria

### M1 — Role Designer

- Create and list roles with name, purpose, responsibilities, required capabilities, and success criteria.
- Reject incomplete submissions without adding a record; preserve entered values for correction.
- Save records locally and reload them after restart.
- Verify validation, capability normalization, and persistence with automated tests.

### M2 — AI Persona Development

- Create and review personas with purpose, working style, strengths, and boundaries.
- Keep personas distinct from roles; one persona may be considered for different roles.
- Persist records; reject incomplete submissions.
- Creating a persona does not provision or execute an agent.

### M3 — Recruitment Office

- Register human candidates and select existing AI personas as potential talent.
- Prepare a consideration referencing one role and one candidate/persona, with evidence and a recommendation.
- Specify the intended Decisions office before submission; reject missing or unknown destinations.
- Preserve the submitted role, participant, evidence, and recommendation as a snapshot.

### M4 — PostOffice and Decisions

- Use the pinned runtime's PostOffice contracts for postal exchange.
- Keep consideration identity, envelope identity, Post Reference, destination, and correlation explicit.
- Distinguish draft, accepted for dispatch, collected by recipient, delivery failure, and decided states.
- Acceptance is custody, never proof of delivery or approval.
- A local Decisions office receives considerations and records an outcome with rationale; Recruitment cannot directly decide its own submissions.
- Return outcomes through Post and correlate them to their original consideration.
- Persist exchange state; retry safely without duplicate considerations or decisions.
- Test destination routing, failures, duplicate delivery, and outcome correlation.

### M5 — Release verification

- Exercise the entire flow for both a human candidate and an AI persona.
- Restart and confirm saved roles, participants, considerations, postal state, and outcomes remain available.
- Show actionable validation and delivery errors in the UI.
- Document local startup, storage/backup, postal guarantees, and the future Forge integration boundary.
- All automated tests and build pass; manually verify the browser flow.

## Boundaries and decisions

- Standalone development continues; do not change or upgrade the runtime submodule to match concurrent Forge work.
- Initial persistence may use a local JSON store for one application process. Do not claim multi-process safety.
- Authentication, payroll, access provisioning, agent execution, recruiting adapters, and automated candidate evaluation are outside v0.1.
- The pinned runtime exposes IPostOffice, IPostmaster, and IPostExchange. No Decisions office implementation was found in this revision. Define the local receiver behind a Talent Campus contract and align with the main campus before integration.
- Destination mapping will be explicit configuration, not guessed from candidate type.
- Local decision recording is a workflow boundary, not an authenticated authorization guarantee. Shared/public deployment requires a separate access-control milestone.

- M4–M5 remain planned; v0.1 is not released.
